Prevention & Protection

🚫

Eliminate Breeding

Remove stagnant water from containers, flower pots, and coolers

🛏️

Use Mosquito Nets

Sleep under insecticide-treated mosquito nets

👕

Wear Protective Clothing

Wear long-sleeved shirts and long pants during peak mosquito hours

🧴

Use Repellents

Apply mosquito repellent on exposed skin and clothing

🏠

Secure Your Home

Use window and door screens to keep mosquitoes out

🌡️

Monitor Symptoms

Watch for high fever, severe headache, pain behind eyes

Symptoms & Emergency Care

🦠 Dengue Symptoms

  • High fever (104°F/40°C)
  • Severe headache
  • Pain behind the eyes
  • Muscle and joint pain
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Skin rash
  • Mild bleeding from nose or gums

🚨 Severe Dengue Warning Signs

  • Severe abdominal pain
  • Persistent vomiting
  • Rapid breathing
  • Bleeding gums
  • Fatigue and restlessness
  • Blood in vomit or stool

Seek IMMEDIATE medical attention if any warning signs appear!

💊 Home Care Tips

1. Stay Hydrated: Drink plenty of fluids - water, ORS, coconut water
2. Rest: Get adequate rest and avoid physical exertion
3. Medication: Use paracetamol for fever and pain. Avoid aspirin and ibuprofen
4. Monitor: Keep track of temperature and symptoms daily
