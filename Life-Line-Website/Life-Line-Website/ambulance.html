🚨 Emergency Instructions While Waiting

For Heart Attack

  • Keep patient calm and seated
  • Loosen tight clothing
  • Don't give anything to eat or drink
  • Be ready with medical history

For Accident Victims

  • Don't move the patient unnecessarily
  • Stop bleeding with clean cloth
  • Keep neck and spine straight
  • Cover with blanket to prevent shock

For Breathing Problems

  • Help patient sit upright
  • Loosen clothing around neck
  • Use inhaler if available
  • Keep calm and reassure patient
