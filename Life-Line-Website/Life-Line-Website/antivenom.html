🚑 Snake Bite First Aid

1

Stay Calm

Keep the patient calm and still. Movement can spread venom faster.

2

Remove Jewelry

Remove rings, watches, and tight clothing from the affected area.

3

Position Properly

Keep the bite area below heart level if possible.

4

Seek Immediate Help

Go to the nearest hospital with antivenom facility immediately.

⚠️

DO NOT:

  • Cut the wound or try to suck out venom
  • Apply ice or tourniquet
  • Give alcohol or pain medication
  • Wait for symptoms to appear
